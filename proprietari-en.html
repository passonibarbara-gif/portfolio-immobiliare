The problem

Own a property in Sicily but live elsewhere?

Between documents to renew, deadlines not to forget and maintenance to follow up, managing a property remotely is complicated. One missed issue is enough to create serious problems.

Without a property manager

  • Tax, insurance or administrative deadlines can easily slip through
  • You don't know who's really checking the property when it's empty
  • Maintenance handled late, or not handled at all
  • No simple way to verify the work that’s been done

With us

  • We track documents and deadlines and warn you in time
  • Regular periodic checks, even when the property is empty
  • Scheduled work, with quotes for you to approve
  • Every job is verifiable from your private dashboard

FAQ

Everything you want to know

How much does the management service cost?

The cost depends on the type of property and the level of service required. After a free assessment we’ll propose a clear rate, with no hidden costs.

Do I need to handle cleaning or maintenance myself?

No, we coordinate cleaning and maintenance ourselves. For major work you’ll always receive a quote to approve before it’s carried out.

Can I check how my property is doing at any time?

Yes, you have access to a private dashboard with occupancy, bookings, expenses, earnings and every job carried out, always up to date.

I live outside Sicily, can I still entrust you with my property?

Yes, that’s actually the most common case: we’re on site and manage everything, you follow progress and verify the work remotely.

How do you handle documents and deadlines?

We monitor insurance, certifications and requirements for the property and warn you well in advance of every deadline, so you never risk forgetting one.

Am I obliged to rent out the property?

No. You can entrust us with just the custody and management (documents, deadlines, maintenance) or, if you wish, ask us to handle rental and guests too.

Is there a minimum contract duration?

Contract terms are agreed together during activation of the service, based on your needs.
